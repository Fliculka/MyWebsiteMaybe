Demo of a visual novel, that was originally made for the Terra event, but had to be put on hold, as the coding part is more or less over, and there is a "30% of time can be art" limit, so I wouldn't have what to do the next 7h. All art made by me + coding done by cat master.
The story follows a young witch, who is stuck in a village for a while. That wouldn't be a problem, if they werent currently on a witchhunt, as they think someone cursed them. She needs to blend in enough so no one would suspect that she's a witch. When the villagers find out, she can use her power of time travel to go back to the time she last woke up. That won't save her every time tho.. will it...
playable in browser - it even should be mobile friendly (only on wide mode)
