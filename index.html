Why and when did I even start drawing you may ask.. Well, I drew a lot when I was little (I drew so many dragons and weird critters T-T), and I kinda became known as "the artist" by the people around me, so I kept it up. My passion to get better and actually start "studying" basics and such came after Covid. That's when I started drawing digitally (which was what fired up that passion combined with a low amount of friends = a lot of alone time) Since then, I joined ArtFight (Best event of the year fr fr). I have a lot of oc's, but I rly enjoy drawing other people's characters too, so I often do freebies on discord (ttzewos art caffe). Lately I started enjoying character design way more, so I make a lot of adopts too.
My art style's consistency is none-existant. I can draw completely differently day from day, it's mainly based on the latest artworks I've seen which gave me motivation to draw that day. I manly draw humans and humanoids. I have nothing against furries, I just can't draw them for the life of me... Maybe one day...
